Meet the cast

Every 10 seconds each agent weighs its options (its goal, live air data, who it trusts, its personality) and acts. Nobody scripts the ending.

Play with the agents

Real life earns seeds. Seeds win showdowns. Games heal the planet for everyone on your street.

00

Smog Siege

The flagship, in 3D: smog bombs rain on the planet. Spin it, blast them, grab golden seeds, and beat the Smog Baron boss with your AI guardians zapping beside you.

01

Trash Dash

Real-time arcade co-op: you and Ripplet (an AI teammate) catch falling litter while the Smog Baron bombs you. Combos, golden seeds, 45 seconds.

02

Baron Duel

Leaf · Wave · Fire with a guardian on your team. The Baron learns your favourite move; your teammate reads his pattern out loud.

03

Live showdowns

The Baron bribes Sunfox in real time. Every phone gets the card. Outbid him with seeds before Sunfox decides, or shield the zone he's eyeing.

04

Litter Hunt

A real walk with GPS and photo proof. Heals the ocean, earns seeds, and puts you on the street leaderboard.

On your phone

Drag the planet, tap a character, jump into a showdown. Hover to pause.

Slides

Use the arrow keys or swipe. Press F for full screen.

How we used our sponsors

Each one is a real call in the running game, and each one powers a core part of it. The app shows a sponsor as live only after its call worked.
